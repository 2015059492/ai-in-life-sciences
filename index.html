AI Integration in Life Sciences Teaching

Discover tools, lessons, and interactive modules designed to help South African educators confidently integrate AI in the classroom

Scroll

Target Groups

In-Service Teachers

These are practicing Grade 10–12 Life Sciences teachers currently working in South African schools. Many are teaching full timetables, often in resource-constrained environments, and may have limited time to learn new technologies. This course supports them by offering practical, time-saving AI strategies they can use immediately in their lesson planning, assessment, and classroom teaching.

Pre-Service Teachers

These are student teachers who are still completing their teaching qualifications. They may be familiar with digital tools but are still learning how to apply them professionally in real classrooms. This course helps pre-service teachers gain confidence with AI before entering the workforce, giving them a strong foundation in modern teaching practices and CAPS-aligned digital integration.
